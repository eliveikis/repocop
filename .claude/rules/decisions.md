# Decision records

Records in `specs/decisions/` are history, and the specs are the truth (`.claude/rules/specs.md` › Decision records). So:

- Don't list or browse `specs/decisions/`, except to number a new record. Open a record by the id a spec, change or rule cites, and only before changing what it decided.
- Never load the records' archive, `specs/decisions/archive/`.
