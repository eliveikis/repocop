How this works

{% if not scope %}Only requirements in progress or in review show here, from every repository; each repository's page shows all of its own. {% endif %}Each requirement is in the first stage that applies, read from the repository itself and GitHub, however the work was done. Delivered: archived in the past week, in the checkout or on the default branch on origin as last fetched. In review: a branch named after it has an open pull request. In progress: such a branch has commits that default branch lacks, or a session works on it. To do: everything else in its requirements folder, in the order they can start. A branch is named after a requirement when its name is the requirement's, alone or after its last /, as bulk-import and agent/bulk-import are for bulk-import.md. Branches are as of the checkout's last fetch.
